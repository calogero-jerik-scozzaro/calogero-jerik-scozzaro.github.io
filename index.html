Research interests

Human reading

Eye tracking in NLP

I use eye movements to study reading difficulty and differences between readers. My work includes reader embeddings for personalised reading-time prediction and CERVINO, an Italian eye-tracking dataset of legal texts.

Reader modelling ↓
Language modelling

Transformer models

I am a co-first author of AlBERTurin, a fully open family of Italian encoders with modern architectures. My interests include pre-training, model evaluation, and perplexity-based classification.
